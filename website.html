About

I work on living wages in global supply chains. At IDH I built the first version of the Salary Matrix, now used by more than 5,000 producers, and coordinated work on the Roadmap on Living Wages.

Today I advise companies, funders and international organizations as an independent consultant, based in Honduras. I studied at Cornell ILR and the LSE Marshall Institute.
