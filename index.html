Funding that brings others in

The best philanthropy doesn’t just pay for change. It brings others to the table to pay for it too.

Some of the most effective work I’ve seen started with a simple idea: public donors combining resources in a shared fund, with a mandate to co-fund alongside companies. At IDH, this approach made it possible to develop new tools, launch sector initiatives and convene whole industries around living wages.

I want more philanthropists to use this model. I help funders design co-funding and catalytic approaches that move whole sectors, not just single projects.

About

Today I advise companies, funders and international organizations as an independent consultant, based in Honduras. I studied at Cornell ILR and the LSE Marshall Institute.

I work through my own consulting firm, Strong LLC. Recent clients include the UN Global Compact and GIZ.
